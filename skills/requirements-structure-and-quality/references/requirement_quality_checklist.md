# Requirement quality checklist

Use this checklist to review whether a requirement is strong enough for downstream use.

## Core checks
- Is the statement clear and specific?
- Is the actor or subject explicit?
- Is the trigger or condition explicit?
- Is the expected outcome explicit?
- Is the statement testable or verifiable?
- Is it free of mixed concerns that should be split?
- Does it avoid vague words such as fast, convenient, flexible, robust, easy, improved?
- Is it consistent with known constraints and other requirements?
- Is its business relevance understandable?

## Common quality failures
- no clear actor;
- no condition or trigger;
- no measurable or observable outcome;
- hidden assumptions;
- multiple requirements packed into one sentence;
- requirement mixed with implementation bias without justification;
- requirement that is really a business question, risk, or idea.
