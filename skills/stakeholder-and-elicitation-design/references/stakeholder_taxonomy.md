# Stakeholder taxonomy

Use this reference to avoid a shallow or one-dimensional stakeholder map.

## Common stakeholder groups

1. Decision and sponsorship
- sponsor
- budget owner
- initiative owner
- governance approver

2. Operational ownership
- process owner
- team lead
- service owner
- platform owner
- compliance or control owner

3. Execution and delivery
- frontline staff
- analysts
- engineers
- support teams
- vendors or partners

4. End-use and impact
- primary end users
- secondary users
- customers or clients
- adjacent teams affected by the change

5. Risk and control
- security
- legal
- compliance
- finance control
- audit or quality assurance

## Practical segmentation dimensions

For each stakeholder, estimate:
- role in the current system;
- role in the change decision;
- expected knowledge contribution;
- influence or veto power;
- impact severity if the change goes wrong;
- likely incentives, concerns, or resistance;
- current level of access and availability.

## Warning signs

A stakeholder map is probably weak if it only names:
- executive roles,
- only direct users,
- only the team requesting the change,
- no control or support functions,
- no owner of exceptions, data, or downstream consequences.
