# Prioritization rubric

Use prioritization to support explainable decisions.

## Common dimensions
- business importance
- urgency or timing sensitivity
- dependency criticality
- risk if delayed
- control or compliance significance
- effort or coordination burden

## Guidance
- Explain why something is high or low priority.
- Note when a lower-value change must move first because it unlocks a higher-value change.
- Separate urgency from strategic importance.
