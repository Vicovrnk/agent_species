# Change impact heuristics

Assess impact across multiple surfaces, not only the changed text.

## Impact surfaces
- scope and business behavior
- process and operating model
- ownership and approvals
- policy or compliance exposure
- data or integration behavior
- reporting and audit implications
- sequencing and release dependency

## Guidance
- Direct impact: the changed requirement or its immediate linked items.
- Indirect impact: affected downstream logic, process steps, or controls.
- Unknown impact: areas where links or evidence are missing.

Do not collapse these into one undifferentiated risk statement.
