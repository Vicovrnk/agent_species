# RICE / WSJF / MoSCoW / Kano guide

## Practical rule
These frameworks help structure discussion, but none should replace judgment.

## Use them when
- the team already understands the method;
- the criteria are stable enough to apply consistently;
- the output needs explainable structure more than exact prediction.

## Avoid misuse
- do not present formula output as truth;
- do not compare unlike items without first shaping them into comparable units;
- do not use a method as camouflage for a pre-decided answer.
